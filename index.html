ResellHQ Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 16, 2026

ResellHQ is a software application designed to help resellers manage inventory, marketplace listings, sales, expenses, and related business information.

Information We Access

When you connect an eBay account to ResellHQ, ResellHQ may access information authorized through eBay's APIs, including account information, listings, inventory, orders, sales, shipping information, and other marketplace data necessary to provide ResellHQ's features.

How We Use Information

Information obtained from eBay is used only to provide and improve ResellHQ functionality, including importing and managing listings, inventory, orders, sales, and business analytics.

Data Sharing

ResellHQ does not sell personal information or eBay account information to third parties.

Data Security

ResellHQ takes reasonable measures to protect information processed through the application. Access credentials and authorization tokens should be handled securely and used only for authorized application functions.

eBay Account Authorization

Users may revoke ResellHQ's access to their eBay account through their eBay account settings. Revoking authorization prevents ResellHQ from accessing additional eBay account information through the authorized connection.

Data Retention

Information is retained only as necessary to provide ResellHQ services, meet operational requirements, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ResellHQ's features and services develop. Updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the ResellHQ application administrator.
